# portfolio W.I.P

New Moon Mining Company (newmoonmining.co): Jack Bowman portfolio, work in progress.

- Open index.html in a browser. On load the letters of jpg / mp3 / mp4 get thrown into the bag (a few bounce back out) and a music note misses, clips the bag and lands beside it (tap it for SoundCloud). Scroll, swipe or use the arrow keys to send the letters out of the bag and back. Click the background to step through the palette.
- Two layouts in one file, picked by orientation: landscape is the big build (bag fills the window, words in front), portrait is the small build (whole bag low, words in a row behind it). Each has its own motion settings in the MOTION block.
- Target devices: iPhone portrait 393×852 and MacBook landscape 1512×982; iPad portrait 820×1180 must not break.
