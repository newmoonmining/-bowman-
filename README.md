# portfolio W.I.P

New Moon Mining Company (newmoonmining.co): Jack Bowman portfolio, work in progress.

- Open index.html in a browser. On load the menu letters get thrown into the bag (a few bounce back out), a music note misses and lands beside it (tap it for SoundCloud), and a wrench pops out to the corner. Scroll to send the words out of the bag; scroll back to put them away.
- The wrench (bottom left) is the settings button: password, then drag anything to move it, drag a corner to resize, tap it for sliders. The bar beside it has undo, text (typeface and colour), bg (palette), edit (text and images, contents only) and export.
- Portrait and landscape are fully separate: layout, type, colour and motion are each set per orientation.
